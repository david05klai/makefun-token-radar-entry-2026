Read the model

A disciplined
starting point.

TVL × 0.25 × maturity × growth

Maturity ranges from 0.65 to 1 over three years since tracking began. Growth is weekly TVL change clamped to −50% to +100%, divided by 250 and added to 1. Missing growth uses zero; unknown age uses 365 days.

The watchlist is supplied by the sealed snapshot; other records without a recorded symbol or CoinGecko ID are exploratory. The data is not proof that a token does not exist. There is no live data, investment advice, or promise of an airdrop.
